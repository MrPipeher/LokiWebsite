Complete Manual

Step-by-Step Knowledgebase

Comprehensive instructions for mastering every system configuration and advanced mode in Loki.

Step 00

Window Placement & Corner-Grip Resizing

Positioning: Left-click and hold anywhere on the dark background of the main overlay window, then drag it to your preferred position on your monitor.

Resizing: To prevent accidental resizing during examinations, the window border is locked by default. Open the System Configuration window (Alt + O or the Gear icon). Once open, hover over the bottom-right corner, click and drag the grip to your desired size, then click Apply Changes.

Steps 01–03

Capturing, Multi-Shot Queuing & Deletion

Instant 3s Hold: Hold Right Mouse Button (RMB) anywhere for 3 seconds and release. It automatically snapshots and queries the AI immediately.

Multi-Shot Queuing: For long questions with separate charts or spread-out options, press Alt + C up to 3 times to queue multiple screens. A red counter badge will appear over the trash icon. Press Alt + Enter to solve all frames together.

Deleting Mis-captures: Press Alt + Backspace or click the Trash icon to remove the most recent capture.

Steps 08–09

Short Answers vs. Step-by-Step Proofs & Math Flipping

Response Depth: Open Settings (Alt + O) > General tab. Check Short Answers for rapid multiple-choice solutions. Uncheck it for deep, pedagogical, step-by-step mathematical proofs and reasoning.

Upside Down Math Inversion: If virtual screen scaling renders LaTeX math upside down, click the Rotate icon or press Alt + F to invert equations vertically.

Steps 10–13

Stealth Modes, Text-Only & Visibility Customization

HUD Opacity: Under General > HUD Opacity, adjust the slider between 0% (glass-clear) and 100% (solid dark slate).

Show Text Only: Checking Show Text Only completely removes the window's dark container, allowing the AI solution to float directly on your screen surface. Pair this with Black text for light backgrounds or White text for dark themes.

Hide Sidebar: Uncheck Show Sidebar Menu to completely collapse the icon rail. You can continue triggering all actions via hotkeys.

Steps 14–15

Custom AI Engine & Keybind Remapping

BYOK AI Engine: Open Settings > Engine tab > check Enable Custom AI. Choose your endpoint provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT, Anthropic Claude, OpenRouter, Zhipu GLM) and paste your API key. You can also paste specific course notes into the Study Notes / Context box.

Keybinds: Under the Keybinds tab, click any shortcut button (it will display [Press a key...]) and type your new single key or modifier combo (e.g. Ctrl + Shift + S), then hit Apply Changes.

Steps 17–18

Automated Archiving & Review Location

Enable Archiving: Check Save Questions under General > Archiving.

Finding Your Questions: Open Windows File Explorer (Win + E) and paste the following path into the address bar:

C:\Users\Public\Documents\TFD_Review

Files are automatically cataloged by date (e.g. [07-06-2026]) and question index (e.g. Question_1.html). Double-click any file to open a full offline review sheet in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
