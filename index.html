FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The quick answers before you get started. Still stuck on something? Our team is one message away on Discord.

Which operating systems are supported?

Loki runs natively on Windows 11 (64-bit) and macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel). Native builds mean no browser dependencies and no performance overhead.

Is Loki really invisible on screen shares and recordings?

Yes. Loki uses hardware-level window exclusion, so the overlay renders on your display only. It is excluded from screen capture, screen sharing, Zoom, Teams, Discord streams, OBS, and capture cards.

Do I need my own AI API key?

Loki is BYOK (Bring Your Own Key). You connect your own key from OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or OpenRouter, which keeps costs transparent and lets you pick the model that fits your subject and budget.

Can I use one license on multiple computers?

Each license is personal and locked to a single machine. Your key automatically binds to the first computer it is activated on, which keeps licenses from being shared or resold.

Can I use Loki on exams or standardized tests?

No. Loki does not work inside secure, lock-down, or proctored browsers and is not permitted on official standardized testing platforms such as Bluebook, the Digital SAT, PSAT, AP Exams, ACT, GRE, MCAT, or LSAT. Always follow the rules of the platform you are using.

What happens when my plan ends?

Access simply expires at the end of your plan. You can renew at any time from the pricing section, and every session you have already archived stays saved locally on your machine.

How do I get support?

Join the Discord community and open a priority support ticket. Setup help, model configuration, and troubleshooting are all handled there.
